About this demo

A small window into OrientMe.

This static prototype demonstrates how Search, Ask, and Orient could help someone find approved work notes and inspect the evidence behind an answer.

  • All source notes are fictional.
  • Search runs in your browser against four bundled examples.
  • Ask and Orient show scripted examples. They do not call a model.
  • No folder selection, account, server, or external data connection is active.
◈ For product review only
